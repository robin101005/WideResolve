# TeleNet Customer Resolution Policies

## Section 1: Billing Inquiries & Adjustments
- **Billing §1.1**: Erroneous Equipment Charges — If unreturned hardware (modem, fiber ONT, TV box) is charged after courier tracking confirms warehouse drop-off, the fee shall be credited back in full within 2 business days.
- **Billing §1.2**: Plan Change Proration — Disputes regarding mid-cycle plan switches shall be recalculated on a strict prorated daily basis.

## Section 2: Network Outages & SLA Credits
- **Outage §2.1**: Short Network Outages — Outages verified in network operations center (NOC) logs exceeding 12 hours qualify for a prorated daily billing credit ($5.00 minimum).
- **Outage §2.2**: Major Outages (>36 Hours) — When residential or business service is interrupted for more than 36 continuous hours as logged by system telemetry, issue a flat courtesy service credit of $35.00 on the customer's next bill.

## Section 3: Compensation & Authority Limits
- **Limit §3.1**: Automated Agent Authority — The automated agent has an authority limit of $40.00 for billing credits. Credits between $40.01 and $100.00 require Level-1 supervisor approval.
- **Limit §3.2**: Credit-Only Policy — All TeleNet resolutions are issued as invoice statement credits, unless the customer account has undergone formal termination.
- **Limit §3.3**: Frequent Claim Review — Customers filing 3 or more tickets within 30 days must be transferred directly to Tier-2 retention specialists.
