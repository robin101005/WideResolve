# QuickCart Customer Resolution Policies

## Section 1: Refunds and Cancellations
- **Refund §1.1**: Double Charges — If a gateway glitch, checkout timeout, or billing error results in duplicate charges for the same order, the secondary charge shall be automatically refunded in full to the original payment method without requiring item return.
- **Refund §1.2**: Standard Returns — Unopened merchandise returned within 30 days of confirmed delivery qualifies for a 100% merchandise refund minus a $5.99 return postage fee.
- **Refund §1.3**: Damaged or Defective Goods — Items received broken, defective, or spoiled qualify for an immediate free replacement or full refund up to the order value. Photographic evidence is requested for orders over $50.00.

## Section 2: Delivery & Shipping SLA
- **Delivery §2.1**: Expedited Transit Delays — When an expedited or guaranteed delivery order arrives more than 48 hours past the estimated delivery window due to courier delay, issue a $10.00 store courtesy credit.
- **Delivery §2.2**: Lost in Transit and Delivery Discrepancies — If carrier telemetry marks an order "Delivered" but the customer reports non-receipt, automated courtesy replacement or credit is capped at $50.00. Disputed claims above $50.00 require human agent investigation.

## Section 3: Compensation & Authority Limits
- **Limit §3.1**: Automated Agent Authority — The automated resolution agent may disburse refunds or credits up to $50.00 per incident without supervisor intervention.
- **Limit §3.2**: Maximum Payout Cap — No refund or credit may exceed the total payment captured for the specific order under review. Duplicate refunds for the same transaction are strictly blocked.
- **Limit §3.3**: Multiple Returns — Accounts with 3 or more dispute filings within a rolling 30-day window cannot be resolved autonomously and must route to human fraud review.
