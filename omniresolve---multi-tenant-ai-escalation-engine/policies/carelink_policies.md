# CareLink Health Resolution Policies

## Section 1: Billing & Copay Reconciliation
- **Billing §1.1**: Duplicate Copay or Service Charges — If a patient is billed twice for the same clinic encounter, specialist consultation, or diagnostic lab test, the duplicate charge shall be refunded immediately to the original HSA/FSA or credit card.
- **Billing §1.2**: In-Network Preventive Care Correction — Preventive wellness screenings inadvertently billed with a copay shall be re-coded and refunded in full up to $60.00 upon confirmation of in-network eligibility.

## Section 2: Scheduling & Administrative Adjustments
- **Scheduling §2.1**: Provider Cancellation Fee Waiver — Late cancellation or missed appointment fees will be automatically waived when caused by clinic scheduling conflicts or verified medical emergencies.
- **Scheduling §2.2**: Administrative Rescheduling — Disputed booking errors will be prioritized for rebooking with an in-network provider within 72 hours.

## Section 3: Authority Limits
- **Limit §3.1**: Automated Financial Cap — The automated billing engine may approve administrative refunds up to $60.00. Amounts above $60.00 require billing manager sign-off.
- **Limit §3.2**: Prior Authorization Boundaries — Disputed charges exceeding $200.00 or involving third-party commercial insurance denial cannot be adjusted without human claims review.

## Section 4: Clinical Safety Firewall (MANDATORY HUMAN ESCALATION)
- **Safety §4.1**: Clinical Consultation Prohibition — The automated system is restricted strictly to financial billing and appointment scheduling inquiries.
- **Safety §4.2**: Immediate Clinical Escalation — Any customer statement mentioning physical symptoms, pain, adverse reactions, medications, dosages, prescriptions, mental health concerns, or emergency medical conditions MUST immediately escalate to human clinical nursing staff. No automated guidance, reassurance, or clinical commentary is permitted.
